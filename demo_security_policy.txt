TRUSTVAULT ZERO-TRUST INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY & CRYPTOGRAPHIC STANDARD
================================================================================

Standard: NIST SP 800-207 Zero Trust Architecture & FIPS 180-4 SHA-256 Chaining

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This document outlines cryptographic standards, access grant lifecycles, and
tamper-evident audit integrity mechanisms enforcing zero-trust data protection
across all corporate vault repositories.

2. CRYPTOGRAPHIC PROVENANCE
All files ingested into TrustVault undergo:
  a. Pre-ingest magic-byte signature validation (MIME-spoofing rejection).
  b. Cryptographic SHA-256 digest computation before persistent storage.
  c. Immutable, sequential hash-chain event logging with monotonic sequence IDs.

3. ACCESS CONTROLS & RECOVERY
Access grants are strictly non-transitive. Revocation triggers instant cache
invalidation and an immediate audit event hash chain entry.
